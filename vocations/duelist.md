# Duelist

[!badge variant="primary" text="Melee"] [!badge variant="danger" text="Dual Wield"]

Aggressive melee fighter with the highest HP growth and native **dual wield** — equip a weapon in each hand.

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| HP gain per level | 15 |
| Mana gain per level | 5 |
| HP regen | 3 HP / 2s |
| Mana regen | 1 mana / 2s |
| Dual wield | Yes |
| Magic shield | No |

---

## Ascendancy Classes

### Berserker

[!badge variant="danger" text="Rage"] [!badge variant="warning" text="Life Leech"]

The Berserker thrives on the edge of death. As their HP drops, they **deal more damage and attack faster** — making low health a weapon. Berserkers also have **passive life leech**, sustaining themselves through combat by draining life with every strike.

### Warden

[!badge variant="primary" text="Tank"] [!badge variant="info" text="Shield Scaling"]

The Warden is a defensive fighter who **deals bonus damage that scales with their shield's defense value**. They build **Bulwark** stacks by blocking attacks, unlocking progressively stronger bonuses.

**Bulwark Stacks** (max 50):

| Stacks | Bonus |
| --- | --- |
| 10+ | +5% block chance |
| 25+ | +10% block chance, reflect 10% of damage taken |
| 50 | +15% block chance, reflect 20% of damage taken |

Reflected damage gains a flat bonus equal to **(shield defense × 0.5)**. Stacks expire after 15 seconds without a block.
