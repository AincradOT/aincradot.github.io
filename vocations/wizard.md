# Wizard

[!badge variant="danger" text="Magic"] [!badge variant="warning" text="DPS"]

Magic user with the highest mana growth and a built-in magic shield. Wizards deal through spells and area magic.

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| HP gain per level | 5 |
| Mana gain per level | 30 |
| HP regen | 1 HP / 2s |
| Mana regen | 3 mana / 2s |
| Magic shield | Yes |

---

## Ascendancy Classes

### Elementalist

[!badge variant="danger" text="Elemental Magic"]

The Elementalist commands multiple elements and can swap between them depending on the situation. Their attacks benefit from **elemental penetration**, allowing them to cut through enemy resistances more effectively than other magic classes.

### Priest

[!badge variant="success" text="Support"] [!badge variant="info" text="Holy Magic"]

The Priest supports and heals the group through dedicated healing spells and buffs, while also wielding holy magic to defend against enemies in combat.
