# Archer

[!badge variant="success" text="Ranged"] [!badge variant="info" text="Distance"]

Ranged specialist with balanced HP and mana growth. Archers deal physical and distance damage from range.

## Stats

| Stat | Value |
| --- | --- |
| HP gain per level | 10 |
| Mana gain per level | 15 |
| HP regen | 2 HP / 2s |
| Mana regen | 2 mana / 2s |
| Magic shield | No |

---

## Ascendancy Classes

### Zenbow

[!badge variant="success" text="Elemental Arrows"] [!badge variant="warning" text="AoE"]

The Zenbow is a mystical archer who empowers auto-attacks with elemental arrow buffs.

**Passive — Zen Focus:** 20% chance to immediately fire a second attack after the first. The second hit also triggers the active arrow buff's effects.

### Sharpshooter

[!badge variant="primary" text="Precision"] [!badge variant="danger" text="Range Scaling"]

The Sharpshooter is a long-range precision fighter who **gains increased damage based on distance** — the further away the target, the harder the hit.
